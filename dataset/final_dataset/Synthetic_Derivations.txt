Synthetic derivations and justification

1) Costs:
- Base USD estimates taken from WHO/WorldBank ranges.
- PHC enforced to be approx 1/5 of private as requested.
- Converted to INR at 83 INR/USD.

2) Travel times -> distances:
- Urban distance km = (urban_time_min / 60) * 30 km/h
- Rural distance km = (rural_time_min / 60) * 22 km/h (adjusted for terrain)
- Values rounded to 0.1 km.

3) Facility counts:
- Where exact counts unavailable, used government reports when possible; otherwise regional estimates with conservative rounding.

4) Arrival rates:
- mean_daily_arrivals = cases_total / 30.

All synthetic fields are flagged in files or noted.
